    >We're honoured to announce a massive program to celebrate Ben Hession's life and contributions to the creative community, with an all day showcase of live music and poetry at UOW UniBar on Saturday March 14 in tribute to our friend.

    Dozens of artists, poets, performers and members of the community have offered up their time and talents to make this happen. The absolutely overwhelming response reflects the kind of dedication Ben inspired, and is a testament to his lasting impact on the local scene.
